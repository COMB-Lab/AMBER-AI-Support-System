On Fri, May 15, 2020, Homeo Morphism wrote:

>I had AmberTools 19.09. update_amber command only took me as far as
>updating to 19.12, but refuses to go further and update to version 20.

That is correct: there is no way to "upgrade" from AmberTools19 to
AmberTools20. You need to download the new tarball. It will un-tar
into an amber20_src folder, so won't interfere with any current Amber
that you have (which should be in folder headed with "amber18").
