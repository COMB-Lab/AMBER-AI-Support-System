Dear Sruthi,

I think you need to ask yourself what you want to learn. You asked essentially if the plot is reasonable. We don’t know. It depends on the science you examining. Are you asking if cpptraj is calculating the value correctly? Have you looked at your trajectory with visualization software? Have you observed structurally what is occurring? Did you place your ligand correctly? Are you using a reasonable force field for the ligand? Is your system sensitive to ions or solvent? If you restart your simulation with a different seed number, do you see the same phenomenon? Is the ligand supposed to dissociate? Is there is a bad contact between your ligand and DNA?

These are all questions only you as the investigator can answer. If you have a technical issue using cpptraj, then we encourage you to look in the tutorials, read the manual and post here if you have something you can’t solve.

Maria
