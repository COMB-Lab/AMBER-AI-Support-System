Hi,

Carlos is right, what you're seeing (the instantaneous jumps) are
almost certainly imaging artifacts. The 'autoimage' command in cpptraj
is usually good at fixing these. E.g.

parm <topology>
trajin <trajectory>
autoimage
rms first <other keywords>

-Dan
