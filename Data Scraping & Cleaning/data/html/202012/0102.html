This seems to happen in larger systems with a high number of solvent and solute. In the left figure, say 200 solutes and 30000 water (a number of other molecules are included in the system making water:non-water about 50/50 by volume). In the right figure, results are from a simulated titration. Is this a problem based on values calculated producing some strange behaviour or is this a problem with imaging and analyses?
