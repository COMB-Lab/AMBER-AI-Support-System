Hi,

If you mean RMSD of every frame in trajectory traj1 to trajectory
traj2 you can use cpptraj, e.g.:

parm myparm.parm7
trajin traj1.nc
createcrd crd1
run
rms2d crdset crd1 <mask> rmsout rmsd1.dat reftraj ref.nc

-Dan
