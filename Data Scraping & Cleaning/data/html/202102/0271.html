Try first with just a couple of atoms in your ref.RST file like:

&rst
         iat=2709,1365,
         r2=72.67,
         r2a=0.0,
         rk2=7.2,
&end

to see if it works.

Second, are you sure you want to have r2a = 0.0 ? that will try to have the two gropus of atoms get to ZERO distance, which I believe will (and should) break everything).

Adrian
