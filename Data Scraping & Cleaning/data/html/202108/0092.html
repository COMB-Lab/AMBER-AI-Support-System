Like Carlos said, you need:

Minimization with Cartesian restraints for the solute
&cntrl
  imin=1, maxcyc=50000,
  ntmin=1, ncyc= 10000,
  ntpr=5,
  ntr=1,
  
  restraint_wt=25, // Cartesian restraint
  restraintmask=':1-492',
&end
