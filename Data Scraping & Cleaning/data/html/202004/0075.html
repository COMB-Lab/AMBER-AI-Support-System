Hi Paulius,

AMBER18 does support 12-6-4 for GPU TI. My experience is that for production, NVT condition can reproduce previous CPU TI results for metal ion solvation free energy. So if you use AMBER18, I’d suggest you use NVT in the production run.

Best,
