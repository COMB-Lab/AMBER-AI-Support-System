Sorry for the delay on this. The answer is pretty straightforward
actually - the "raw" RDF is literally that - just the total bin
counts, so if you're doing more than one frame you'll easily get more
than the total number of waters in the system. Try it on a single
frame to see what I mean. Hope this helps,
