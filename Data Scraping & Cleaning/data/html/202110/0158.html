The actual error message says nothing useful, other than that an error
occurred. If it happens at the beginning of MD, you probably have some bad
contacts or other problems, even with a partially minimized structure.
Do a short run (just a 100 MD steps) with ntpr=1, and see if you can learn
anything.

You may need to do more minimization (with the CPU), or run the short MD
described above on a CPU.
