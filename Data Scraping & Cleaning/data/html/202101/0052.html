Dave Cerutti pointed you to mdgx, but (for me) it's much simpler just
to use the cpptraj "check" command: it will print out short non-bonded
contacts and bad bonds (the two most common problems with initial
structures.)

cpptraj <prmtop file>
trajin <inpcrd file>
check
