Hi,

You can actually use pmemd.cuda for heating/equilibration (I'm not sure
about minimization). From a workflow POV, it works fine if you don't think
your system will blow up in heating and have a stable box density volume.
If it doesn't, you get an error message about the periodic box changing too
much. In the case it happens, you can just restart till it works, or just
shift to pmemd till the box volume/density equilibrates out.

Best,

Kenneth
