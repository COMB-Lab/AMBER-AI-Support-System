I believe you want the plane-plane angle restraint from the nmr restraints
module. See 27.1.1 of the amber 20 manual (p546).
It even mentions that if you want to define the planes with 3 atoms each
instead of 4 you can just repeat one of the atoms in each plane.
Hope that helps.
