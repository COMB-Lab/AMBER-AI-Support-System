Hi,

You could just do 2 extra energy calculations and subtract them out:

energy Eligand :164
energy Ereceptor :1-163
energy Etotal :1-164
run
Einteraction = Etotal - Ereceptor - Eligand
writedata Einteraction.dat Einteraction

Hope this helps,

-Dan
