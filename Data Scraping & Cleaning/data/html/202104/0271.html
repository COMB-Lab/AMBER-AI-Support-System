These warnings are just letting you know that the NetCDF trajectory
file from REMD doesn't have a 'temperature' dimension, as was the case
in the past. This is the reason you couldn't use ptraj to process
these trajectories. If you didn't receive any error messages things
seem to have worked fine. Let me know if not.
