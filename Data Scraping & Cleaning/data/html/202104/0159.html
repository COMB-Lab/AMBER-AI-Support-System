I don't think it's very feasible to use quasiharmonic calculations to
estimate entropy on a 234-residue protein. It's probably possible to make
some approximations (e.g. don't compute all the modes), but this would be a
research project in itself, and one would need to understand in detail what
quasiharmonic calculations are doing.
