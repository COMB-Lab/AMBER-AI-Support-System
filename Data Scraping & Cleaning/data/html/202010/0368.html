Exxact conducted AMBER 20 GPU Benchmarks on a number of NVIDIA GPUs.

For testing, we used an Exxact AMBER Certified MD system, running Amber20 benchmarks using the following GPUs: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3080, NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3090, NVIDIA A100, Quadro RTX 6000, and the RTX 2080 Ti.

All benchmarks were performed using a single GPU configuration using Amber 20 Update 6 & AmberTools 20 Update 9. NVIDIA CUDA 11.1 was also used for these benchmarks.

Highlights of our results showed:


  * Ampere GPUs (RTX 3090, RTX 3080 & A100) outperformed all Turing models (2080 Ti & RTX 6000) across the board.
  * For the larger simulations, such as STMV Production NPT 4fs, the A100 outperformed all others.
  * For smaller simulations the RTX 3090 and RTX 3080 showed excellent performance, and in some cases on par with the A100.
