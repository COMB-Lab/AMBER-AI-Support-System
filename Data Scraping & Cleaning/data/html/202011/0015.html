>I just ran a 100 step minimization to see how the NMR restraints are

>found this section on restraints.
>Is this getting printed out on the out file because I have set LISTIN=POUT?

Yes. It looks like you should be getting non-zero NMR energies. Run a
1-step minimization, and study both the LISTIN (inoming) and LISTOUT
(final) sections carefully. Try without TI to see that you can get the
energies you want from the simplest calculation, in particular, with no
softcore region. [Interactions between softcore and non-softcore atoms
are treated in a special way, but I'm reaching here.]

The main goal is to find a simple calculation where the NMR energies are
what you expect, then slowly morph things to the calculation you want,
in order to pin down the problem. None of these tests should require
more than just looking at what happens at the first point. You could
even run a zero-step MD just to see if there could be some discrepancy
between minimization and MD.
