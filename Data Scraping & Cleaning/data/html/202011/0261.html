So in terms of the original Cremer & Pople 1975 paper, the first
column cpptraj prints out is "phi2" (the pseudorotation angle), the
second (with aspect [Amp]) is "Q" (the amplitude), and the third (with
aspect [Theta]) is "theta"; all reported in degrees.
