Hi,

You will need cpptraj version 5.0.1 or greater from GitHub, or cpptraj
from AmberTools 21 (which is version 5.1) to avoid these warnings.
However, if you're not writing any topologies (i.e. you're just doing
coordinate analysis) you can ignore these warnings.

-Dan
