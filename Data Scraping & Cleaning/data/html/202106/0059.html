since it's complaining about missing parameters, you might check the part
of the output that you didn't include, such as the response from leap when
you tell it to load the files that should have these parameters. Did you
previously have a leaprc file in the directory where you ran it, and do not
any more? I suggest checking the parts that load parameters, such as the
leap input, the leaprc, and possibly your AMBERHOME.
