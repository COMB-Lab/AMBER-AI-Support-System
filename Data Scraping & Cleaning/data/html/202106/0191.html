Hi,

Sounds like it could be a visualization artifact. To see if it is, try
running the minimization with coordinate wrapping off (i.e. 'iwrap=0')
or use cpptraj to reimage the coordinates/restart (via e.g. the
'autoimage' command).

Hope this helps,

-Dan
