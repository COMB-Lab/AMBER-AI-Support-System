The energy function only takes 1 mask. You could do it with 3 separate
energy calculations, total, peptide, and water, then subtract peptide and
water from total:

energy Total ...
energy Peptide :1-19 ...
energy Water :WAT
run
Inter = Total - Peptide - Water
writedata inter.dat Inter
