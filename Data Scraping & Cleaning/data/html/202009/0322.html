Yes, works on my end as well. Good one!

Using a combination of xlsfonts, xfd and grep you can pretty much pick a font you like and then just make alias entries in your profile for separate fonts
    alias times_leap="xleap -fn '*times-bold-r*150*’"
    alias courier_leap="xleap -fn '*times-bold-r*150*’”

and so on.
