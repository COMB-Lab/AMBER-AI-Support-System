> I have identified that the error source is the command “autoimage origin”

This isn't an error. You've simply imaged the system in a different
way. With periodic boundary conditions, both pictures are equivalent.
The 'autoimage' command by default will try to center the picture on
the first molecule. Based on the results from 'autoimage', it seems
like you may need more of a buffer in your system (unless for some
reason you need that particular concentration).

-Dan
