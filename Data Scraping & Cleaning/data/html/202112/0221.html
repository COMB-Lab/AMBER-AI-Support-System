The origin is the set of coordinates of the lower grid corner. I.e., the most negative point along the x, y and z axes.

To the get the center of box (which may be the center of mass of the solute, depending on your choice of `centering`), multiply the number of grid points by the delta and add it to your origin. E.g., in your case the center of the box along the x-axis is

-18.08241552 + 240 * 0.5 = 101.9175845

Hope this helps,
