Thanks there Carlos,

Changing the r1-r4 values finally made it work, looks like there had to be a large enough distance between r1 and r2 (and r3 and r4). I had changed it to all 1s after the direct output from cpptraj giving a distance of 0.5 between r1 and r2 didn’t work. Now the distance between r1 and r2 is 30, the restraint is working pretty OK at 10kcal/mol/A.
