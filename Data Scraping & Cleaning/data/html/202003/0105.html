The file format is here: http://ambermd.org/FileFormats.php#frcmod
When you look at the frcmod, you will see the different categories of
parameters (Mass, Bond, Angle, dihedrals, etc). It will be easier if the
file you are loading into tleap with the loadAmberParams command is in that
format. Therefore, your angles should be under the section ANGLE, your
dihedrals should be under your section DIHEDRAL, etc. This should fix the
warning you are getting. For example, HW OW HW 100 104.52 should be
under the ANGLE section.

You can look at your frcmod parameters and compare them to the parameters
in the loadAmberParams file.
