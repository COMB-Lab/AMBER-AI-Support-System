Hi,

The error arises because 'out' is not a recognized command. What did
you intend with 'out remap.dat'? By itself, 'out' is not a command,
though it is a keyword for many other commands. Perhaps what you
wanted instead of out was parmwrite out <file> to write a topology?

-Dan
