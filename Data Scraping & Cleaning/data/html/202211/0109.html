S-(dimethylarsenic)cysteine is a non-standard (not “non-specific”) amino acid. Your choice is to either find parameters in the literature or to develop parameters yourself. Googling "AMBER, parameterize non-standard residue” returns some great references for a starting place.

I hope that helps.
