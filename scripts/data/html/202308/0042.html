We are currently searching for a Postdoctoral Scientist to contribute to an exciting NIH-funded initiative focused on advancing a cutting-edge next-generation simulation package, OpenAMBER.

 

Responsibilities for this role include:

 

* Collaborating closely with both academic and industrial partners to conduct simulations for various drug-discovery endeavors.
* Developing and maintaining comprehensive software-requirement documents for the innovative simulation package, OpenAMBER.
* Thoroughly conducting beta tests and documenting precise test records and reports for newly integrated features in both the current AMBER and the upcoming OpenAMBER simulation packages.

 

This position is particularly well-suited for individuals with career aspirations as application scientists within the dynamic field of drug discovery.

 

For more detailed information, please visit https://www.openamber.org/home/career .

 

To apply, please submit your cover letter, CV including a list of your publications, as well as the names and contact information of 2-4 references, to the Project Principal Investigator:

 

Prof. Tai-Sung Lee

Email: taisung.rutgers.edu

 

The OpenAMBER Project Team
